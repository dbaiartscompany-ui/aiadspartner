Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Ads Partner ("we", "us") handles information when you visit aiadspartner.com or use our services. We keep this simple, because our data practices are simple.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you book a strategy call or email us, we receive the details you provide — typically your name, email address, business name, and anything you choose to share about your goals.
  • Scheduling data. Calls are booked through Calendly. Information you enter there is processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may log standard technical information (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes.

The interactive scanner on our homepage runs entirely in your browser. Anything you type into it is not transmitted to us or stored by us.

2. How we use information

  • To schedule and conduct strategy calls and deliver audit services you request.
  • To respond to your inquiries.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run our business (such as scheduling, email, and hosting providers), or where required by law. These providers process data on our behalf and under their own security obligations.

4. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above — typically for the duration of our communication or engagement with you, plus any period required for legal or accounting purposes. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the UK/EU GDPR and applicable US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@aiadspartner.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

This website does not set marketing or analytics cookies of its own. Embedded third-party services (such as Calendly, if you open the booking page) may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

7. International transfers

We serve clients in the United States and United Kingdom, and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
